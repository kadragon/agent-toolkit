# Routing — siblings, cross-cutting lessons, and deferral

Load this when a lesson could belong to more than one asset, or when a routing decision is
tempted toward "later". `SKILL.md` owns both rules; this file holds two edge cases.

**Attribution.** Reflecting from the session's tool record rather than recall, and checking a
target's siblings before routing a lesson to one of them, are adapted from **Task Observer ("One Skill to Rule Them All") by Eoghan Henn, rebelytics.com**,
https://github.com/rebelytics/one-skill-to-rule-them-all, licensed **CC BY 4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

*Changes made in this adaptation:* the persistent cross-session observation log, its numbering
and archival protocol, the weekly review, the skill-authoring and staging rules, the
handoff-doc mode, and the in-session capture log are **not** carried over — this repo's
cross-session mining lives in `dev:harness-curate` and its skill authoring in `skill-creator`.
No text is copied verbatim.

## Edge cases

- **Self-declared generic rule.** A rule that declares itself generic inside one artefact ("this
  applies to any file-writing script, not just X") is the cheapest possible propagation signal —
  treat that phrasing as an automatic multi-asset flag.
- **A deferral criterion must be able to fire.** Ask who would have to act for it to fire, and
  whether they have reason to do the opposite. A deferral whose criterion cannot fire is a
  silent drop wearing a precise phrasing.
